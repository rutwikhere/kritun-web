LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy for Kritun

Last updated: December 2025 Kritun (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Kritun mobile application (the “App”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App. We are committed to maintaining the privacy, security, and integrity of your data.

01

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you access or interact with the App:

a. Personal Information

  • Name or username: Used to create and identify your gamer profile.
  • Profile photo: Displayed on your user profile, posts, and team lineups.
  • Email address: Managed securely via Firebase Authentication for account access and verification.

b. User-Generated Content

  • Posts, stories, and comments: Highlights, Krits, and discussions you publish.
  • Messages and chat content: Communications sent within direct chats and squad channels.
  • Images and media: Screenshots, gameplay clips, and profile images uploaded by users.

c. Device & Usage Information

  • Device type and operating system: Collected for compatibility, rendering, and in-app update delivery.
  • App usage data: Diagnostics, crash reports, and performance analytics to improve stability.
02

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Provide, maintain, and deliver the core functionality of the App.
  • Enable gaming social features including Krits posts, stories, team challenges, and real-time chat.
  • Improve app performance, responsiveness, and user experience across diverse devices.
  • Send high-priority notifications related to app activity, team invitations, and match alerts.
  • Ensure safety, enforce community guidelines, and prevent platform misuse or abusive behavior.
03

3. Data Storage and Security

  • User data is securely stored and processed using industry-standard third-party infrastructure including Google Firebase and Supabase.
  • We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
  • While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
04

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • To comply with applicable laws, legal obligations, court orders, or governmental requests.
  • With trusted service providers (Firebase, Supabase) strictly necessary for delivering authentication, database, storage, and push notification infrastructure.
05

5. User Content & Moderation

  • Users retain responsibility for the content, text, and media they upload or distribute through the App.
  • We reserve the right to review, moderate, and remove any content that violates community safety standards or applicable laws.
  • Users have the ability to delete their own posts, comments, and stories at any time directly within the App.
06

6. Children’s Privacy

Kritun is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our servers.

07

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have control over your data and account settings:

  • Edit or delete content: Modify your profile, game IDs, or remove your Krits and stories.
  • Account deletion: Request complete deletion of your account and associated records by contacting us.
  • Notification controls: Configure or revoke push notification permissions via device settings or in-app team options.
08

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates or modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

09
